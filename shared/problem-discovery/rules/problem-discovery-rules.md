# Problem Discovery: Evidence and Experiment Rules

## Non-negotiable boundaries

1. A reported complaint is a **signal**, not an observed operational problem. An AI-generated idea, speculative survey answer, forum JSON tree, or unchecked source URL is **not** primary behavioral evidence. Label each claim with its provenance, observation date, evidence level, uncertainty, and verification needed next. Never invent citations, observations, interviews, competitor checks, or field visits. Search queries generated by the bundled CLI are suggestions, not executed research.
2. Preserve distinct states for **research score**, **primary evidence audited**, **experiment completed**, **experiment passed**, and **validation**. A 28–35/35 score means high research priority only. A claim of validation requires a preregistered trial that meets its recorded numeric metric and failure rule, plus a manually reviewed local observation artifact. Do not mark a candidate validated from `--experiment-outcome` prose, arbitrary source strings, unreviewed artifacts, ratings, or hypothetical willingness to pay.
3. A SHA-256 digest establishes that a local file's bytes match a record; it does **not** establish authenticity, representativeness, a real user, or that the claim follows from its contents. A human reviewer must examine the underlying observation, redact sensitive information, and record the claim, observation date, reviewer, review date and file digest. If human review cannot be independently corroborated, describe the result as user-supplied and not externally verified.
4. All stages must answer: What is known? What remains unknown? What concrete test resolves the uncertainty? Do not silently skip field interviews, root-cause analysis, alternative audits, or trials. When a needed observation cannot be performed, explicitly record `NOT_RUN` or `UNVERIFIED`.

## Research and evaluation procedure

Follow the seven-stage sequence from `skills/SKILL.md`: domain/track -> signal mining -> 14-node workflow reconstruction -> alternatives and non-software sufficiency -> 35-point **research** score -> preregistered small experiment -> requirements-based smallest solution and dossier export.

- Track A (`commercial`) evaluates historically demonstrated spend, purchaser control, frequency, and economic consequence. Track B (`free_utility`) evaluates accessibility, useful recurrence, and time saved; zero WTP is expected and is not a reason to reject a free utility.
- Evidence levels: L1 directly observed behavior and operational artifacts; L2 dated first-hand interviews with specific historical numbers; L3 independent reviews/forum sentiment (including JSON comment trees); L4 secondary interpretations; L5 speculation. L4 scores are capped at 1 per dimension, L5 scores at 0. A number-only score or L3 sentiment cannot independently establish primary behavioral proof.
- Stop checks may park candidates for low consequence, sufficient alternatives, single-source bias, internal policy/training causes or an unreachable audience. **No workaround** demands an inaction-root-cause check: distinguish triviality from expensive or inaccessible alternatives. Do not automatically reject a sector from a brand-name keyword; evaluate exact operator workflow and realistic competitive fit.
- Before building software, check whether an existing spreadsheet, checklist, standard operating procedure or WhatsApp template resolves the measured need adequately. If so, document `non_software_sufficient: true` and prefer that fix.
- Do not infer architecture or price from title/domain tokens. Populate `solution_constraints` from observed requirements (UI, local batch, browser-page integration, shared data, persistence, background jobs, offline). With unknown constraints leave solution undecided; with server requirements document why a hosted backend is necessary. Record human review before final scope selection.
- A trial needs a dated preregistered hypothesis, explicit failure rule, metric, threshold/direction, test dates, sample size, numeric observed result, evidence artifact and dated named manual review. If it misses threshold, record `EXPERIMENT_FAILED` and revise or park; an absent or unverifiable contract is not validation.

## Storage and reporting

Write dossiers only to a user-selected accessible location (`--export-obsidian /path/to/discovery_logs`) and rebuild CSV from the notes. Do not hardcode one person's home directory. The central CSV must preserve actual `validation_status`, `evidence_verified`, `experiment_completed`, and `experiment_passed` separately from `priority_score`. Existing notes without an auditable experiment must not be promoted to validation by their score or a legacy label.

If the environment has no field access, no consent, no readable artifacts, or no target vault, report that limitation and give the next local verification step. Do not claim the agent performed a physical visit, audited a file, ran searches, or wrote to an unavailable mount.
